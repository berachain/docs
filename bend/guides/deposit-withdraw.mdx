---

Follow these steps to deposit into and withdraw from a Bend vault.

## Requirements

- Wallet with \$BUSD
- Native \$BERA for transaction fees

<Tip>
  If you don't have \$BUSD, get it on [Berachain Hub](https://hub.berachain.com/swap) or [BUSD Swap](https://busd.berachain.com).
</Tip>

## Depositing

Deposit \$BUSD to earn native lending yield and PoL reward yield.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit Bend and connect wallet">
    Go to [Bend](https://bend.berachain.com/lend) and connect your wallet.

    <Frame>
      ![Bend deposit connect wallet](/images/bend/deposit-connect-wallet.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a vault">
    Choose a vault to deposit \$BUSD into.

    <Frame>
      ![Bend choose vault](/images/bend/deposit-choose-vault.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure deposit">
    1. Select **Supply**
    2. Enter the \$BUSD amount to deposit
    3. Choose **Stake** or **Supply & Stake** (recommended for PoL reward yield)
    4. Click **Review** to open the confirmation modal

    <Tip>
      You can enable **Supply & Stake** later if you skip it now.
    </Tip>

    <Frame>
      ![Bend configure deposit](/images/bend/deposit-configure-deposit.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve, supply, and stake">
    Your wallet will prompt you to:

    1. Approve the vault to spend your \$BUSD
    2. Supply \$BUSD to the vault
    3. Receive receipt tokens
    4. Stake the receipt tokens in a Berachain PoL Reward Vault for reward yield

    <Frame>
      ![Bend supply and stake](/images/bend/deposit-supply-stake.png)
    </Frame>

    <Frame>
      ![Bend successful supply and stake](/images/bend/deposit-successful-supply-stake.png)
    </Frame>

    You should see your deposited value (supplied and staked) and the receipt token amount.

    <Frame>
      ![Bend staked receipt tokens](/images/bend/deposit-staked-receipt-tokens.png)
    </Frame>

    With your deposit staked, you can claim PoL rewards.

    <Frame>
      ![Bend deposit yields](/images/bend/deposit-yields.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Withdrawing

Unstake and withdraw your \$BUSD.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure withdrawal">
    In the vault you want to withdraw from:

    1. Select **Withdraw**
    2. Enter the \$BUSD amount to withdraw

    <Tip>
      If your balance shows 0, your deposit is staked. Unstake first, then withdraw.
    </Tip>

    <Frame>
      ![Bend configure withdrawal](/images/bend/withdraw-configure-withdraw.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unstake deposit">
    Where your staked balance is shown, click **Unstake**.

    <Frame>
      ![Bend unstake deposit](/images/bend/withdraw-unstake.png)
    </Frame>

    Enter the amount to unstake and confirm.

    <Frame>
      ![Bend confirm unstake](/images/bend/withdraw-confirm-unstake.png)
    </Frame>

    <Frame>
      ![Bend successfully unstaked](/images/bend/withdraw-successfully-unstaked.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Withdraw deposit">
    After unstaking, your balance appears under **Deposited**. Withdraw from there.

    1. Enter the \$BUSD amount to withdraw
    2. Select **Withdraw**

    <Frame>
      ![Bend specify withdrawal amount](/images/bend/withdraw-specify-amount.png)
    </Frame>

    Confirm the withdrawal in your wallet.

    <Frame>
      ![Bend confirm withdrawal](/images/bend/withdraw-confirm-withdrawal.png)
    </Frame>

    When complete, your \$BUSD is back in your wallet.

    <Frame>
      ![Bend successful withdrawal](/images/bend/withdraw-successful-complete.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
