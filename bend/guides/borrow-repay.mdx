---
title: "Borrow & Repay"
description: "Supply collateral, borrow BUSD, monitor LTV/LLTV, and repay to withdraw collateral on Bend."
---

Follow these steps to supply collateral, borrow \$BUSD, and repay to free your collateral.

## Requirements

- Wallet with collateral (WETH, WBERA, WBTC, etc.) listed on [Bend](https://bend.berachain.com/borrow)
- Native \$BERA for transaction fees

<Tip>
  If you don't have collateral, swap on [Berachain Hub](https://hub.berachain.com/swap).
</Tip>

## Supply collateral and borrow

Supply \$\$WBERA as collateral and borrow \$BUSD.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit Bend and connect wallet">
    Go to [Bend](https://bend.berachain.com/borrow) and connect your wallet.

    <Frame>
      ![Bend borrow connect wallet](/images/bend/borrow-connect-wallet.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a market">
    Choose a market where you can supply collateral. This guide uses \$WBERA.

    <Frame>
      ![Bend choose market](/images/bend/borrow-choose-market.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure borrow">
    1. Select **Borrow**
    2. Enter the \$WBERA amount to supply as collateral
    3. Click **Review** to open the confirmation modal

    <Frame>
      ![Bend configure borrow](/images/bend/borrow-configure-borrow.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve and supply">
    1. Approve the market to spend your \$WBERA
    2. Supply \$WBERA as collateral

    <Frame>
      ![Bend approve and supply](/images/bend/borrow-approve-supply.png)
    </Frame>

    <Frame>
      ![Bend successful approve and supply](/images/bend/borrow-successful-approve-supply.png)
    </Frame>

    You should see your supplied collateral and total value in the market.

    <Frame>
      ![Bend successful supply](/images/bend/borrow-successful-supply.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Borrow">
    1. Enter the \$BUSD amount to borrow
    2. Keep borrow below the LTV limit to avoid liquidation
    3. Click **Review** to confirm

    <Frame>
      ![Bend borrow](/images/bend/borrow-borrow.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve and borrow">
    Approve the borrow in your wallet.

    <Frame>
      ![Bend approve borrow](/images/bend/borrow-approve-borrow.png)
    </Frame>

    <Frame>
      ![Bend successful approve borrow](/images/bend/borrow-successful-approve-borrow.png)
    </Frame>

    You should see your borrowed \$BUSD and its value in the market.

    <Frame>
      ![Bend successful borrow](/images/bend/borrow-successful-borrow.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Avoid liquidation">
    Monitor your position. If your LTV exceeds the Liquidation LTV (LLTV), your position can be liquidated.

    <Frame>
      ![Bend monitor LTV and LLTV](/images/bend/borrow-monitor-ltv-lltv.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Repay and withdraw

Repay \$BUSD and withdraw your \$\$WBERA collateral.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure repay">
    1. Select **Repay**
    2. Enter the \$BUSD amount to repay
    3. Enter the \$WBERA amount to withdraw
    4. Click **Review** to open the confirmation modal

    <Frame>
      ![Bend configure repay](/images/bend/repay-configure-repay.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm repay">
    Confirm the repayment in your wallet.

    <Frame>
      ![Bend confirm repay](/images/bend/repay-confirm-repay.png)
    </Frame>

    <Frame>
      ![Bend successful confirm repay](/images/bend/repay-successful-confirm-repay.png)
    </Frame>

    You should see a zero balance after the repayment.

    <Frame>
      ![Bend successful repay](/images/bend/repay-successful-repay.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

You have now supplied collateral, borrowed \$BUSD, and repaid to withdraw your collateral on Bend.
